About Got to Go

Find public toilets across the UK using live data from the Toilet Map.

Privacy: Your location stays on your device. We don’t upload GPS coordinates to our servers.

Data: Contains data from the Toilet Map © Public Convenience Ltd — CC BY 4.0.

Tips: Save favourites with ★. Use filters for accessible, free, RADAR, and open-now toilets.

Ratings are stored only on this device. Report wrong info via Toilet Map on each toilet’s page.
